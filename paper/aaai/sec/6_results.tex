\subsection{Recall--Violation Results}

Table~\ref{tab:main-results} and Figure~\ref{fig:tradeoff} show that
family-aware re-ranking yields a weakly better $K=50$ point-estimate
Recall--Violation trade-off for all three predictors. For both compatibility
estimators, the paired scan-resampling intervals are strictly positive for
Recall and negative for Violation on Open3DSG and SGFN. On VL-SAT, whose Source
Recall is already high, the Recall intervals contain zero while the Violation
intervals remain negative, supporting lower verifier-derived Violation without
a detectable Recall loss. Open3DSG exposes the mismatch most strongly: Source
ranks many geometrically inconsistent candidates near the top, whereas both
\method{} variants raise Recall and sharply lower Violation without applying a
hard filter. Across $K\in\{5,10,20,50,100\}$, neither variant has a lower Recall
point estimate or a higher Violation point estimate than its corresponding
Source baseline for any of the three predictors. SGFN is tied at $K=5$ and
changes only marginally at $K=10$, while Open3DSG shows the largest gains across
the reported budgets.

No single ranking rule dominates across all predictors and reported $K$ values.
At $K=50$, \methodmlp{} raises Open3DSG Recall more than \methodlinear{}
(46.70\% versus 44.18\%) but also has higher Violation (4.13\% versus 3.42\%);
the two variants are nearly tied on VL-SAT and SGFN. Thus model capacity changes
the operating point without identifying a universally superior estimator.
RankAvg and RRF attain competitive Violation in some cells but incur larger
low-$K$ Recall losses on VL-SAT and SGFN. Product (all families) can raise
aggregate Recall and sometimes lower aggregate Violation, but it changes
support/contact selections and can mask a regression in that family. The pooled
family-conditioning ablation is reported in the supplement. Both proposed
variants, RankAvg, and RRF use the same family-aware ranking procedure and
therefore preserve the source relation-family sequence and keep support/contact
candidates in source order; Product (all families) does not impose this
restriction.

\subsection{Point- and Mesh-Based Consistency Audit}

The compatibility estimators and primary verifier share some OBB-derived
measurements. We therefore remeasure proximity and vertical violations from
reconstructed instance geometry using two representations. One estimate uses
robust point-cloud vertex distances and heights; the other uses area-weighted
samples from reconstructed mesh triangles. Thresholds are fixed from
training-split positive relations. Neither estimate reads OBB measurements,
predictor identity, the source relation score, learned compatibility, or primary
verifier status labels.

Table~\ref{tab:surface-audit} reports Violation@$K$ at $K=50$ for
\methodlinear{} using point- and mesh-based labels. A candidate is labeled
satisfied or violated only when the two estimates agree; otherwise it is
uncertain. This measure is distinct from primary Violation and is therefore not
directly comparable to Table~\ref{tab:main-results}. It decreases for all three
predictors, with paired 95\% intervals below zero. The supplement reports both
\method{} variants across all five $K$ values; the point- and mesh-based changes
have the same direction throughout, with SGFN at $K=5$ as the only tie. The
audit does not alter the rankings, so Recall is unchanged from
Table~\ref{tab:main-results}.

\subsection{Ablations and Controls}

For compactness, Table~\ref{tab:ablations} reports structural controls for
\methodlinear{} and includes the full \methodmlp{} operating point at both
budgets. Both are proposed compatibility estimators; the corresponding MLP
controls are reported in the supplement. For the Linear head, wrong-pair and
shuffled geometry reduce Recall and increase Violation for every predictor at
both budgets, showing that the compatibility estimate depends on the
corresponding ordered subject--object pair rather than on the marginal
distribution of geometry measurements. A wrong predicate and an endpoint swap
that keeps the predicate fixed produce the largest Violation increases. Their
nearly identical aggregate values arise because both reverse the signed
interpretation of vertical-order geometry.

For the Linear head, distance-only ordering raises Open3DSG Recall but
substantially increases its Violation, while degrading both metrics on VL-SAT
and SGFN. Compatibility-only ordering preserves or lowers Violation but loses
substantial Recall on VL-SAT and SGFN, showing that compatibility does not
replace the source relation score. The full MLP rows show that nonlinear
capacity reaches a different Recall--Violation operating point without changing
the proposed framework. Feature-removal analyses, linked
positive--counterfactual ordering, transformation checks, and complete matched
controls for both heads are reported in the supplement.
