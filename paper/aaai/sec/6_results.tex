Table~\ref{tab:source-results} asks whether calibrated geometry consistency
reduces violations without collapsing recall across both top-ranked and
standard operating points. Open3DSG exposes the clearest top-rank failure:
\texttt{semantic\_only} has \vAt{5}=0.5131, \vAt{10}=0.3255, and
\vAt{20}=0.2088. \texttt{family\_specific} reduces these to 0.0420, 0.0482,
and 0.0441 while raising \rAt{5}/\rAt{10}/\rAt{20} from
0.0368/0.1002/0.1991 to 0.0984/0.1921/0.3291. At the standard operating
points it gives the strongest Open3DSG tradeoff: \rAt{50}/\rAt{100}=0.4658/0.6047
with \vAt{50}/\vAt{100}=0.0286/0.0341.

VL-SAT provides the controlled anchor: \texttt{probabilistic} improves
\rAt{50}/\rAt{100} from 0.9272/0.9635 to 0.9305/0.9688 and reduces
\vAt{100} from 0.0476 to 0.0404; \texttt{family\_specific} reduces
\vAt{10}/\vAt{20}/\vAt{100} to 0.0051/0.0109/0.0333. The small
VL-SAT recall changes at low $K$ reflect a ceiling-like controlled source,
while violations remain consistently lower. \texttt{rule\_verified} is a
zero-violation diagnostic, not the default method.

A Docker subgraph bootstrap with 1,000 resamples gives the Open3DSG
\texttt{family\_specific} operating point strong top-rank deltas:
\rAt{10}/\rAt{20} improve by +9.19/+12.99 percentage points (95\% CI
+7.84,+10.75 and +11.13,+14.78), while \vAt{10}/\vAt{20} drop by
-27.74/-16.47 points (95\% CI -29.42,-26.08 and -17.35,-15.60). At
\rAt{100}/\vAt{100}, the same condition changes Open3DSG by +8.86 points
(95\% CI +6.69,+10.96) and -9.01 points (95\% CI -9.49,-8.53). VL-SAT deltas
are smaller but favorable at the standard point: \texttt{family\_specific}
changes \rAt{100} by +0.48 points (95\% CI +0.11,+0.93) and reduces
\vAt{100} by -1.43 points (95\% CI -1.60,-1.28). We use these intervals as
evaluation-context uncertainty checks, not as repeated-training variance.

Controls rule out simpler alternatives. On VL-SAT, geometry-only reaches only
\rAt{100}=0.5184 with \vAt{100}=0.0711, distance-only reaches 0.5554 with
0.0981, and shuffled/wrong-pair geometry degrades violations to 0.0588/0.0601.
Open3DSG shows the same pattern: distance-only gives \rAt{100}=0.5038 and
\vAt{100}=0.1071, geometry-only gives 0.5116 and 0.0865, and
shuffled/wrong-pair controls fall to \rAt{100}=0.2543/0.2331 with
\vAt{100}=0.1998/0.1985. Thus \sysname{} is not merely ranking by geometry,
distance, or hard rules; object-pair identity and calibration matter.

The GT-based verifier check tests the geometry signal independently: GT
positives remain nonviolated at 0.9965, counterfactual negatives are
nonsatisfied at 0.9673, and \pgeom{} separates 7,944 rows with AUROC/AUPRC
0.9772/0.9729 and Brier 0.0543. This distinguishes the calibrated reliability
signal from a purely hand-written validity script.
It also limits what can be claimed: \sysname{} measures whether a predicted edge is
consistent with declared geometry evidence; it does not certify unmeasured
functional or language-pragmatic relation correctness.

Structured audit and visual sanity checks support failure interpretation but
are not large-scale human evaluation: a 250-row structured audit reaches
quality-issue precision 0.8933, and a 50-row visual spot-check has target-bucket
quality-issue rate 0.9333 with contradiction rate 0.0333.

Together, the metrics and controls support a calibrated reliability-layer
interpretation, not a new relation generator or broad open-vocabulary SOTA
claim. Open3DSG comparisons share the same selected checkpoint, row contract,
full-validation denominator, and recovered source payload. The historical R2
branch stays outside Table~\ref{tab:source-results}: it covers 388/388 contexts
and changes old 377/388 \rAt{100} by only about +0.28 points, so the old
missing-context caveat did not create the trend. Its clean-return raw files are
row-equivalent after excluding run metadata, although the process-level teardown
exit-137 caveat remains.
The 533/548 unmodified full-validation branch is retained for the same reason:
it follows the Open3DSG source preprocessing pipeline, and the main conclusion
should not depend on hiding the resulting preprocessing visibility drop or on
treating the recovery branch as an unmodified-source result.

Failure analysis explains why the Open3DSG metrics change. The full-validation
branch produces 82,155 diagnostic rows with zero validation errors; in the
36-case queue, 25 cases are demoted by geometry-aware re-ranking.
Figure~\ref{fig:failures} shows distance, vertical-order, and support-plane
contradictions that make symbolic edges unreliable for downstream reasoning.

The same analysis exposes residual risk: eight of 36 sampled rule-violated cases
still have $\pgeom > 0.9$. Thus \pgeom{} is not a hard validity label, and the
paper reports probabilistic, rule-verified, and family-specific variants
separately.
