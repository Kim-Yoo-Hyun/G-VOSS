\section{Model and Preprocessing Details}

\paragraph{Open3DSG.}
Open3DSG uses the official non-averaged BLIP checkpoint
\texttt{epoch=13-step=13104.ckpt}, selected by train/dev validation loss.
Public preprocessing produces candidates for 533/548 contexts; the main
evaluation retains the full 548-context ground-truth universe and treats the
15 contexts without candidates as empty sets. For sensitivity, the eligible-only denominator
contains 3,899 relations, and a documented recovery covers all 548 contexts by
lowering the visible-object minimum to two and regenerating views for two
scans.

\paragraph{SGFN.}
We use the official \texttt{full\_l160} source with its 160-object,
26-relation checkpoint on the same evaluation scans and denominator.
