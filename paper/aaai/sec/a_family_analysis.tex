Support/contact is exactly unchanged because the route preserves both its
selection and the source-ranked family composition at every $K$. Vertical
order drives most of the aggregate reduction. The result is consequently a
scoped operating-point improvement, not a uniform family claim. Full within-family
and global-slice results for $K\in\{5,10,20,50,100\}$, including simultaneous
family-wise intervals, are included in the supplemental package.
