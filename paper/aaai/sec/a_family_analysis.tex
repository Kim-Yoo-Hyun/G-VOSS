\section{Family Composition at the Global $K=100$ Operating Point}

Table~\ref{tab:family-slices} reports each family's slice inside the actual
global top-100 ranking. It therefore exposes both within-family behavior and
changes in the family composition of the selected list. Deltas compare the
family-calibrated product with the source score, using paired subgraph
bootstrap samples.

Support/contact regresses in all three sources, while vertical ordering drives
most of the violation reduction. The aggregate result is consequently a scoped
operating-point improvement, not a uniform family claim. Full within-family
top-$K$ and global-slice tables for $K\in\{5,10,20,50,100\}$ accompany the
submitted artifacts with simultaneous family-wise uncertainty.
