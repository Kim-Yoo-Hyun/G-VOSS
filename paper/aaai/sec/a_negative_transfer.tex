\section{Cross-Dataset Transfer Development Diagnostic}

We evaluate the 11-scene official ReplicaSSG test split with the official
VisualGenome-trained FROSS RT-DETR-EGTR release. The initial result is used to
diagnose and develop source-scale-robust fusion, so selected configurations
are reported as development estimates. The exact
mapping is \texttt{near}$\rightarrow$\texttt{close by},
\texttt{above}$\rightarrow$\texttt{higher than}, and
\texttt{under}$\rightarrow$\texttt{lower than}; support/contact predicates are
excluded because no exact subtype mapping was available. The run contains
4,290 candidates, 172 exact-label ground-truth relations, 11 paired bootstrap units, and
passes all 24 source, mapping, and artifact-integrity checks.

Neither initial GeoCalib instantiation passes. Product is identical to semantic
ranking at the primary endpoint, so it fails the strict violation-improvement
criterion. Rank-average sharply lowers verifier-derived V but fails the recall
guardrail. Product improves secondary $K=20$ and $K=50$ tradeoffs, but these do
not replace the primary $K=100$ result. This failure exposes raw-product
score-scale sensitivity, context saturation at $K=100$, excessive displacement
under rank fusion, geometry-representation shift, and a highly imbalanced
near/above/under ontology. We use these observations for method development
and do not interpret this table as an unbiased dataset-generalization estimate.
V remains verifier-derived rather than independent human physical validity.

\paragraph{Bounded-fusion follow-up.}
The regenerated FROSS execution contains 4,293 candidates, three more than the
initial execution; every condition below uses this same regenerated candidate
set. We evaluate 355 context-quantile and bounded-penalty configurations,
including decoders constrained by
$|\operatorname{rank}_{new}-\operatorname{rank}_{source}|\le B$ for
$B\in\{10,20,50,100\}$. The all-scene choice
$q^Z-[.6-C]_+$ preserves Recall@100 and lowers V, but it is an optimistic
development fit. Selecting on ten scenes and evaluating the held-out scene
loses recall and fails the diagnostic criterion.
