\section{External Transfer Stress Test}

We apply the 3DSSG-trained compatibility model and family-slot rule without
target-specific refitting
to the official ReplicaSSG test scenes using FROSS predictions. No ReplicaSSG
row is used for fitting, normalization, imputation, or hyperparameter selection.
The exact mapping covers \texttt{near}, \texttt{above}, and \texttt{under};
support/contact is excluded because the ontologies do not provide an exact
mapping. We report this as a transfer stress test rather than evidence of
dataset-level generalization.

Paired scene-bootstrap intervals support joint improvement at $K=10$
($\Delta$R [.0048,.1467], $\Delta$V [$-.1000$,$-.0091$]) and $K=50$
($\Delta$R [.0179,.0811], $\Delta$V [$-.0683$,$-.0185$]). The $K=20$
Recall interval touches zero, while $K=5$ is inconclusive. At $K=100$, the
product changes only one selected row: 86.28\% of source scores are exactly
zero, and unresolved boundary ties in 9 of 11 scenes make multiplicative
re-ranking nearly inert. Candidate support also caps exact-label Recall at
76/172 (44.19\%). The stress test therefore supports budget-dependent transfer,
not dataset-level generalization.
