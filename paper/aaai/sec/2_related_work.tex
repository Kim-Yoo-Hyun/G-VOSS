\section{Related Work}
\subsection{3D Scene Graph Prediction}
3D scene graphs organize reconstructed environments as object nodes connected by semantic and spatial relation edges \cite{armeni20193d}.
3RScan provides repeated indoor reconstructions, and 3DSSG adds object--relation annotations and a relation-prediction benchmark \cite{wald2019rio,wald2020learning}. Incremental and online systems study persistent graph construction from RGB-D streams and spatial perception pipelines \cite{wu2021scenegraphfusion,hughes2022hydra}. Edge-based and multimodal methods improve relation features and spatial reasoning \cite{zhang2021exploiting,feng20233d,wang2023vl}. Recent works study self-supervised, object-centric, and edge-centric representations \cite{koch2024sgrec3d,heo2026object,ma2026edge}. These methods and RelCompat3D use reconstructed scene evidence, but they optimize relation generation, whereas RelCompat3D re-ranks fixed relation candidates after prediction.

Open-vocabulary 3D perception provides queryable point and instance features \cite{Peng_2023_CVPR,takmaz2023openmask3d}. Graph systems use open-vocabulary features for querying, planning, navigation, and task-driven interaction \cite{gu2024conceptgraphs,werby2024hierarchical,maggio2024clio}. Recent 3D scene graph methods extend this direction to open-vocabulary objects and relations, online generation, and functional reasoning \cite{koch2024open3dsg,chen2024clip,hou2025fross,zhang2025open,wang2025open}. RelCompat3D asks whether a predicted predicate is supported by the reconstructed geometry of its ordered pair.

\subsection{Geometry-aware Relation Evidence}
3D relation predictors use edge features, metric geometry, and semantic--geometric cues \cite{zhang2021exploiting,feng20233d}. Scene graph alignment uses semantic and geometric structure for matching and registration \cite{sarkar2023sgaligner,xie2024sg}. Adjacent work uses geometry to constrain 3D object placement or ground object affordances \cite{huang2025fireplace,shao2025great}. A recent preprint proposes visual--geometric relation witnesses for open-vocabulary relation learning under incomplete supervision \cite{nguyen2026relwitness}. These approaches and RelCompat3D share the goal of grounding semantic relations in geometric evidence. RelCompat3D differs by excluding predictor identity and the source relation score from compatibility and applying the resulting signal to fixed candidates within each relation family.

Several systems incorporate geometry or transformation into relation generation. GEODE jointly denoises object geometry and relation predicates in an open-vocabulary graph generator \cite{Feng_2026_CVPR}. RelGraphOV uses relation-aware graph construction with geometric pruning \cite{chen2026beyond}. Transformation-Aware Decoupling modifies relation generation for viewpoint robustness \cite{sun2026not}. For fixed predictions from a 2D scene graph generator, Neau et al. apply spatial, functional, and relational constraints through declarative refinement \cite{neau2026visual}. These methods and RelCompat3D all use structured relation evidence. However, RelCompat3D estimates a continuous compatibility score from reconstructed ordered-pair geometry and enforces applicable endpoint/predicate transformations. It evaluates the resulting re-ranking with Recall@$K$ and Violation@$K$.

\subsection{Reliability Evaluation and Calibration}
Confidence calibration asks whether model scores reflect empirical accuracy. Neural network confidence often does not match empirical accuracy and temperature scaling reduces this mismatch without changing class order \cite{guo2017calibration}. Deep ensembles estimate predictive uncertainty \cite{lakshminarayanan2017simple}. Evaluations under distribution shift test whether that uncertainty remains reliable \cite{Ovadia2019CanYT}. Scene graph work models semantic ambiguity~\cite{yang2021probabilistic}, calibrates long-tailed predictions~\cite{zhu2024calibration}, and constructs conformal prediction sets \cite{nag2025conformal}. In 3D, SCR-SSG refines relation scores from masks, neighboring relations, and statistical priors~\cite{yeo2025statistical}. PUF propagates uncertainty during online fusion \cite{yang2026puf}. These methods and RelCompat3D address reliability beyond task accuracy, but RelCompat3D measures predicate--geometry compatibility rather than calibrated correctness or predictive uncertainty.

Recall@$K$ measures exact-match retrieval at a rank cutoff rather than
compatibility with reconstructed ordered-pair geometry. RelCompat3D reports exact-match Recall@$K$ with verifier-derived Violation@$K$ and uses compatibility for within-family re-ranking. Together, these metrics characterize retrieval and geometric compatibility. Neither metric is a calibrated probability.
