\section{Related Work}

\subsection{3D Scene Graph Prediction}

3D scene graphs organize reconstructed environments as object nodes connected
by semantic and spatial relation edges \cite{armeni20193dscenegraph}. 3RScan
provides recurring indoor reconstructions, and 3DSSG adds object--relation
annotations and a relation-prediction benchmark
\cite{wald2019rio,wald2020learning3dssg}. Incremental and online systems study
persistent graph construction from RGB-D streams and spatial perception
pipelines \cite{wu2021scenegraphfusion,hughes2022hydra}. Recent predictors
improve closed-set relations through edge reasoning, point-cloud features,
multimodal cues, spatial knowledge, self-supervised pretraining, and
object- or edge-centric representations
\cite{zhang2021sggpoint,feng2023smka,wang2023vlsat,koch2024sgrec3d,heo2025ocrl3dssg,ma2026leo}.
These methods and \method{} both use reconstructed scene evidence, but they
optimize relation generation, whereas \method{} assesses and re-ranks fixed
relation candidates after prediction.

Open-vocabulary 3D perception provides queryable object and region features
\cite{peng2023openscene,takmaz2023openmask3d}, and graph systems connect such
features to scene representations for querying, planning, navigation, online
mapping, and language interaction
\cite{gu2024conceptgraphs,werby2024hovsg,maggio2024clio}. Recent 3D scene graph
methods extend this direction to open-vocabulary objects, open-set relations,
vision--language model (VLM) features, online graph generation, and functional
relations
\cite{koch2024open3dsg,chen2024ccl3dsgg,hou2025fross,zhang2025openfungraph,wang2025octreegraph}.
These methods broaden the candidate vocabulary and downstream uses; \method{}
instead keeps each predictor fixed and tests whether its selected predicate is
compatible with the reconstructed geometry of the corresponding ordered pair.

\subsection{Geometry-Aware Relation Evidence}

3D relation prediction and graph reuse already exploit edge features, metric
distance, topology, support/contact patterns, and semantic--geometric fusion
\cite{zhang2021sggpoint,feng2023smka,xie2024sgpgm,
huang2025fireplace,shao2025great,sarkar2023sgaligner}. RelWitness learns
open-vocabulary relations from visual--geometric witnesses under incomplete
relation supervision \cite{nguyen2026relwitness}. These approaches and
\method{} share the goal of grounding semantic relations in geometric evidence;
\method{} differs by excluding predictor identity and score from compatibility
and applying the resulting signal to fixed candidates within each relation
family.

Several systems incorporate geometry or transformation structure inside the
relation generator. GEODE jointly denoises object geometry and predicates
\cite{feng2026geode}, RelGraphOV uses relation-aware graph construction and
geometric pruning \cite{chen2026relgraphov}, and Transformation-Aware
Decoupling improves generator robustness to yaw-viewpoint changes
\cite{sun2026tad}. For fixed 2D scene-graph-generation predictions, Neau et al.
mine spatial, functional, and relation-algebra constraints and report a
Constraint Violation Rate after declarative refinement
\cite{neau2026commonsense}. These methods and \method{} all impose structured
relation evidence, but \method{} estimates continuous compatibility from the
reconstructed ordered pair and enforces applicable endpoint/predicate
transformations before joint Recall@$K$--Violation@$K$ evaluation.

\subsection{Reliability Evaluation and Calibration}

Confidence calibration asks whether model scores reflect empirical accuracy.
Temperature scaling adjusts neural confidence without changing the predicted
class order \cite{guo2017calibration}; deep ensembles estimate predictive
uncertainty, evaluation under distribution shift tests its robustness, and
selective prediction trades conditional risk for coverage
\cite{lakshminarayanan2017deepensembles,ovadia2019uncertaintyshift,geifman2017selective}.
Scene-graph work models semantic ambiguity, calibrates long-tailed predictions,
and constructs conformal prediction sets
\cite{yang2021probabilistic,zhu2024longtailcalibration,nag2025conformalsgg}. In
3D, SCR-SSG refines relation scores from masks, neighboring relations, and
statistical priors, while PUF propagates uncertainty during online fusion
\cite{yeo2025scrssg,yang2026puf}. These methods and \method{} both address
reliability beyond task accuracy, but $C^{\rm tr}$ measures
predicate--geometry compatibility rather than calibrated correctness,
predictive uncertainty, or abstention confidence.

Recall@$K$ measures retrieval rank rather than compatibility with reconstructed
ordered-pair geometry. \method{} therefore reports exact-label Recall@$K$
alongside verifier-derived Violation@$K$ and uses compatibility for
within-family re-ranking. This joint evaluation complements conventional
retrieval and calibration analyses; neither metric is a probability guarantee.
