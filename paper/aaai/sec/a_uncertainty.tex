\section{Verifier-Uncertainty Sensitivity}

The primary $\mathrm{V@K}_{all}$ includes uncertain rows in its denominator but
does not label them satisfied. We therefore recompute every evaluated ranking with
$\mathrm{V@K}_{dec}$ over satisfied/violated rows, uncertainty rate $U@K$, and
a pessimistic bound $\mathrm{V@K}_{pess}$ that counts every uncertain row as a
violation. Table~\ref{tab:uncertainty-sensitivity} gives the primary $K=100$
comparison.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\scriptsize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3.5pt}
\caption{\textbf{Uncertainty sensitivity at $K=100$.} Lower is better for all
four reported quantities.}
\label{tab:uncertainty-sensitivity}
\begin{tabular}{llrrrr}
\toprule
Source & Condition & $V_{all}$ & $V_{dec}$ & $U$ & $V_{pess}$ \\
\midrule
VL-SAT & Source score & .0476 & .0729 & .3464 & .3941 \\
VL-SAT & Family product & .0333 & .0485 & .3127 & .3460 \\
\midrule
Open3DSG & Source score & .1242 & .2128 & .4164 & .5406 \\
Open3DSG & Family product & .0341 & .0457 & .2539 & .2880 \\
\midrule
SGFN & Source score & .0630 & .1005 & .3732 & .4362 \\
SGFN & Family product & .0381 & .0577 & .3396 & .3777 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The paired product-minus-source changes in $V_{dec}$ are $-.0244$
[$-.0270$,$-.0220$] for VL-SAT, $-.1671$ [$-.1748$,$-.1597$] for Open3DSG,
and $-.0428$ [$-.0457$,$-.0394$] for SGFN. Uncertainty rate also decreases on
all three sources. The corresponding pessimistic-V changes are $-.0480$,
$-.2526$, and $-.0586$, with all 95\% CIs below zero. Thus the reported
improvement does not rely on promoting uncertain rows. Full $K$ grids and
shared-index paired intervals accompany the submitted artifacts.
