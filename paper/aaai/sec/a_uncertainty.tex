\section{Verifier-Uncertainty Sensitivity}

The reported $\mathrm{V@K}_{all}$ includes uncertain candidates in its denominator but
does not label them satisfied. We therefore recompute each ranking with
$\mathrm{V@K}_{dec}$ over satisfied/violated candidates, uncertainty rate $U@K$, and
a pessimistic bound $\mathrm{V@K}_{pess}$ that counts every uncertain candidate as a
violation. Table~\ref{tab:uncertainty-sensitivity} gives the $K=100$
comparison.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\scriptsize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3.5pt}
\caption{\textbf{Uncertainty sensitivity at $K=100$.} Lower is better for all
four reported quantities.}
\label{tab:uncertainty-sensitivity}
\begin{tabular}{llrrrr}
\toprule
Source & Condition & $V_{all}$ & $V_{dec}$ & $U$ & $V_{pess}$ \\
\midrule
VL-SAT & Source score & .0476 & .0729 & .3464 & .3941 \\
VL-SAT & \method{} & .0295 & .0452 & .3473 & .3768 \\
\midrule
Open3DSG & Source score & .1242 & .2126 & .4161 & .5402 \\
Open3DSG & \method{} & .0324 & .0548 & .4084 & .4408 \\
\midrule
SGFN & Source score & .0630 & .1005 & .3732 & .4362 \\
SGFN & \method{} & .0350 & .0565 & .3803 & .4153 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\method{}-minus-source changes in $V_{dec}$ are $-.0277$ for VL-SAT, $-.1578$
for Open3DSG, and $-.0440$ for SGFN; their paired 95\% intervals all exclude
zero. Uncertainty-rate changes are +.0009, $-.0077$, and +.0071, whereas
pessimistic-V changes are $-.0173$, $-.0994$, and $-.0209$. Thus both
decidable-only and pessimistic V are lower for all three predictors even though
uncertainty does not decrease uniformly.
The hard-rule baseline has zero Violation when the denominator includes all
verifier statuses, but it retains uncertain candidates and therefore has pessimistic
V of .3712/.4722/.4042. Thus zero measured violation
does not imply reliable or complete selection.
