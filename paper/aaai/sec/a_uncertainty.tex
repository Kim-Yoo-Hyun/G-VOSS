\section{Verifier-Uncertainty Sensitivity}

The primary $\mathrm{V@K}_{all}$ includes uncertain rows in its denominator but
does not label them satisfied. We therefore recompute each ranking with
$\mathrm{V@K}_{dec}$ over satisfied/violated rows, uncertainty rate $U@K$, and
a pessimistic bound $\mathrm{V@K}_{pess}$ that counts every uncertain row as a
violation. Table~\ref{tab:uncertainty-sensitivity} gives the primary $K=100$
comparison.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\scriptsize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3.5pt}
\caption{\textbf{Uncertainty sensitivity at $K=100$.} Lower is better for all
four reported quantities.}
\label{tab:uncertainty-sensitivity}
\begin{tabular}{llrrrr}
\toprule
Source & Condition & $V_{all}$ & $V_{dec}$ & $U$ & $V_{pess}$ \\
\midrule

\midrule

\midrule

\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Algebra-product minus source-score changes in $V_{dec}$ are $-.0256$
[$-.0282$,$-.0229$] for VL-SAT, $-.1663$ [$-.1736$,$-.1594$] for Open3DSG,
and $-.0442$ [$-.0472$,$-.0409$] for SGFN. Uncertainty-rate changes are
$-.0341$, $-.1450$, and $-.0340$; pessimistic-V changes are $-.0493$,
$-.2354$, and $-.0597$. All corresponding paired 95\% intervals exclude zero.
The hard-rule baseline has zero all-status V but retains uncertain rows and
therefore has pessimistic V of .3712/.4722/.4042. Thus zero measured violation
does not imply reliable or complete selection.
