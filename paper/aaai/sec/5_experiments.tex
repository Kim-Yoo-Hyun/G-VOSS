\section{Experimental Setup}

This section fixes scope before results because \method{}'s claim depends on
denominator transparency as much as metric values. Open3DSG is the main
open-vocabulary relation-source case study, while VL-SAT is a controlled
reproduced closed-set anchor. Families and denominators are fixed before
source-result reporting, yielding a predeclared physically checkable slice:
157 scans, 548 contexts, 36,808 directed pairs, 957,008 VL-SAT prediction rows,
11,254 ground-truth rows, and 3,972 in-scope GT relations. Table~\ref{tab:scope}
records the denominator and source outputs used by Table~\ref{tab:source-results}.

Open3DSG is reported as a Docker-reproduced source-output reliability case
study, not an official leaderboard reproduction. The selected checkpoint is the
official non-averaged BLIP route \texttt{epoch=13-step=13104.ckpt}, chosen by
train-dev validation loss before held-out source-result inspection. Its runtime uses
filtered train/dev subgraphs, and the main full-validation branch reaches
548/548 contexts with \texttt{OPEN3DSG\_MIN\_VISIBLE\_OBJECTS=2} plus relaxed
view regeneration for two scans. The 533/548 covered branch and historical
old 377/388 vs R2 388/388 branch remain sensitivity evidence only.

Condition names are fixed before reporting. The calibrated product
\texttt{family\_conditional\_risk} ranks by
$\mathrm{score}^{sem}\cdot\pgeomfam$; rank-average combines within-subgraph
semantic and family-geometry percentiles with fixed equal weight.
\texttt{probabilistic\_recalibrated} is the pooled calibrated-risk ablation
$\mathrm{score}^{sem}\cdot\pgeom$. RRF is a fixed strong comparison, while
the calibrator-only control ranks by \pgeom{} without source confidence. It is
not true raw-$G$-only because the frozen calibrator retains predicate/family
and predicate-aligned interaction features.

Calibration inputs are classified as $T$, raw predicate-independent $G$, or
$T\times G$; $Z$ and source identity are excluded. A separate strict train-only
factor audit does not alter the frozen SGFN gate.

SGFN \texttt{full\_l160} is the prospective exact-label target. Its source
test list exactly matches the frozen 157-scan H001 scope. The target, K=100,
families, denominator, calibrated product, rank-average, RRF, and bootstrap
gates were fixed before correct-checkpoint download and inference. Two
pre-inference errata corrected the split identity and an initially mistaken
\texttt{l20} checkpoint URL; all versions remain in provenance. SGFN covers all
36,808 nonself directed pairs. Eleven self-\texttt{supported by} GT rows have no
model edge, receive no synthesized prediction, and remain in the 3,972-row
recall denominator.

Open3DSG and VL-SAT retain the case-study/anchor roles above. The untouched
ReplicaSSG/FROSS transfer stress test is negative evidence (supplement), while
Qwen-VL remains optional. Broad 3DSSG generation gains are not claimed. All
promoted results use Docker; host-only outputs are excluded.

\paragraph{Blinded Codex LLM proxy audit.}
Following LLM annotator/evaluator and 3D-judge precedent
\cite{gilardi2023chatgpt,liu2023geval,wu2024gpt4vision}, two locked Codex passes
label 488 items from 137 scans as
\texttt{valid}/\texttt{invalid}/\texttt{ambiguous}/\texttt{unobservable}
from blinded relation/pair evidence; pass two does not see pass one before lock.
Their shared model family makes this a proxy stability test, not human evidence.
