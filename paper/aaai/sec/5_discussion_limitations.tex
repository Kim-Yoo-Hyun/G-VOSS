\section{Discussion and Limitations}

Open3DSG~\cite{koch2024open3dsg} uses text-embedding cosine similarity, which does not explicitly estimate predicate--geometry compatibility, whereas VL-SAT~\cite{wang2023vl} and SGFN~\cite{wu2021scenegraphfusion} use task-specific relation scores. Across both score types, RelCompat3D estimates compatibility without predictor identity or source scores, providing a predictor-agnostic reliability signal for grounded relation ranking. Beyond 3D scene graphs, the framework could extend to open-vocabulary spatial relation retrieval, relation-aware scene alignment, and embodied relation grounding when candidates have corresponding ordered-pair geometry.

The evidence covers one 3DSSG validation split~\cite{wald2020learning} and assumes known instances with reconstructed ordered-pair geometry. Support/contact candidates remain in source order. The point- and mesh-based audit uses the same scenes and ontology, making it an alternative measurement rather than independent ground truth. Broader claims require independent labels, richer contact and pose evidence, and additional datasets.
