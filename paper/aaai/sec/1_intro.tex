\section{Introduction}

3D Scene Graphs turn reconstructed scenes into structured object-relation
representations that can support spatial reasoning, embodied AI, scene
alignment, visual grounding, and downstream decision making
\cite{wald2020learning3dssg,sarkar2023sgaligner,xie2024sgpgm,liu2026viewongraph}.
For these uses, relation prediction must be supported by the reconstructed
scene, not only plausible at the category or language level. A graph edge such as
\texttt{standing on}, \texttt{next to}, or \texttt{higher than} is useful only
if it describes the actual subject-object pair in 3D.

We study a failure mode in which relation predictors rank plausible labels that
are inconsistent under explicit tests of the actual object-pair geometry.
A relation can sound plausible for two object categories while being
contradicted by their contact evidence, distance, or vertical arrangement in
the reconstructed scene. This mismatch matters more as 3D Scene Graphs become
interfaces for language-facing, open-vocabulary, and embodied systems
\cite{koch2024open3dsg,hou2025fross,zhang2025openfungraph,wang2025octreegraph,saxena2025zing3d,yu2025openworld3dsg,madhavaram2026vizor}.

The failure is not simply that existing 3DSSG methods ignore geometry. Prior
work already uses edge features, point-cloud structure, spatial knowledge,
multimodal semantics, and geometric fusion
\cite{zhang2021sggpoint,feng2023smka,wang2023vlsat,koch2024sgrec3d,chen2024ccl3dsgg}.
The gap is more specific: a source relation score---even when its predictor
already uses geometry---is not necessarily an explicit estimate of whether the
same subject-object pair satisfies the predicate in 3D. This makes the
problem an AI reliability issue, not only a 3DSSG implementation detail:
language-facing and embodied systems can inherit a confident symbolic relation
that contradicts the underlying world state.

The resulting design requirement is factor isolation. \method{} treats an existing
predictor as a relation source, standardizes its outputs into
identity-preserving prediction rows, joins object-pair geometry evidence by
subject and object instance identifiers, and separates predicate/family
semantics $T$, predicate-independent same-pair geometry $G$, and source score
$Z$. A source-independent compatibility factor
$C=P(y^{cal}=1\mid T,G)$ is calibrated without $Z$; the source score enters
only in the final ranking $F(Z,C)$. This separation makes the mechanism
falsifiable: wrong predicates, wrong pairs, and shuffled geometry should
destroy compatibility while source confidence remains unchanged. A
family-calibrated product preserves source-score
magnitudes while penalizing family-conditioned geometry risk; rank-average
combines semantic and geometry percentiles without assuming shared source
scales. Pooled compatibility calibration is an ablation, Reciprocal Rank
Fusion is a strong comparison, and hard-rule filtering is a diagnostic rather
than the proposed operating point.

We evaluate support/contact, proximity, and relative-vertical relations because
their constraints are identifiable from reconstructed ordered-pair geometry.
Open3DSG supplies the main open-vocabulary case study, VL-SAT a controlled
closed-set anchor, and SGFN an additional source evaluation
\cite{wang2023vlsat,koch2024open3dsg}. The evaluation reports exact-label
recall together with verifier violation, uncertainty, coverage, paired
confidence intervals, and family composition. ReplicaSSG/FROSS provides a
cross-dataset transfer stress test and development diagnostic for source-scale
and ranking-transfer failures.

Our contributions are threefold:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item We identify and measure a post-source failure: a semantically
    confident relation can contradict the reconstructed geometry of its actual
    ordered object pair. We expose it through a joint exact-label recall,
    verifier-violation, uncertainty, and coverage contract.
    \item We introduce a factor-isolated reliability layer that estimates
    predicate-conditioned compatibility $C(T,G)$ under $Z\notin C$ and combines
    it with source confidence only during re-ranking. Identity,
    wrong-predicate, and transformation controls make the mechanism falsifiable.
    \item We establish source-level generalization across VL-SAT, Open3DSG, and
    SGFN under one geometry-identifiable 3DSSG target, and expose family
    heterogeneity and cross-dataset transfer failures rather than averaging
    them away.
\end{enumerate}

The calibrated product improves the recall--violation operating point on the
failure-exposing Open3DSG source, preserves a near-saturated VL-SAT anchor, and
satisfies the primary joint criterion on SGFN. These results establish a reliability
layer for geometry-checkable relations rather than a new relation generator.
