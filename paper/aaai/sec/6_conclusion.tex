\section{Conclusion}

RelCompat3D learns predicate--geometry compatibility without source relation scores or predictor identity, then combines it with source relation scores during family-aware re-ranking. Across three predictors on the shared 3DSSG validation scenes, both variants show lower or tied verifier-derived Violation point estimates while preserving or improving Recall point estimates at every reported $K$. Compatibility-only ordering nearly matches RelCompat3D-Linear on Open3DSG, while source relation scores remain important for Recall on VL-SAT and SGFN. These results support predicate--geometry compatibility as a predictor-agnostic reliability signal that complements, rather than uniformly replaces, source relation scores.
