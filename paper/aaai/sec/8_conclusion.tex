\section{Conclusion}

GeoCalib isolates source confidence from predicate-conditioned object-pair
geometry. Across three predictors on a fixed 3DSSG target, this separation
improves the aggregate recall--violation trade-off, and the calibrated product
persists across smaller ranking budgets. GeoCalib contributes a source-
independent reliability framework with explicit family and transfer limits,
not a universally optimal fusion rule.
