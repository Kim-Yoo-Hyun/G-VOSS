\section{Problem Formulation}

Let a relation source produce a set of candidate relation rows for a 3D scene.
Each row is written as
\begin{equation}
r_i = (\mathrm{scan}_i,\mathrm{context}_i,s_i,o_i,p_i,a_i,Z_i),
\end{equation}
where $s_i$ and $o_i$ are subject and object instance identifiers, $p_i$ is the
predicate label, $a_i$ is the mapped predicate family, and $Z_i$ is the source
relation score. The tuple
$(\mathrm{scan}_i,\mathrm{context}_i,s_i,o_i)$ is the identity key used to
preserve the object pair across prediction, geometry, and ground-truth joins.
We expose predicate semantics separately:
\begin{equation}
T_i=(p_i,a_i),
\end{equation}
while withholding $Z_i$ from geometry compatibility calibration.

For each prediction row, we join raw object-pair geometry evidence
\begin{equation}
G_i = G(\mathrm{scan}_i,\mathrm{context}_i,s_i,o_i).
\end{equation}
The raw evidence is predicate-independent and includes oriented bounding-box
features, local contact evidence, pair distance and scale, vertical ordering,
and observability indicators. \method{} evaluates only geometry-checkable
relation families:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{A}=\{&\mathrm{support/contact},\ \mathrm{proximity},\\
              &\mathrm{vertical\ order}\}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Rows outside these families are excluded from the measured violation claim.

The geometry verifier assigns a row-level status
$v_i \in \{\mathrm{satisfied},\mathrm{uncertain},\mathrm{violated}\}$. The
calibrator learns compatibility against a constructed calibration target:
\begin{equation}
C_i=P(y_i^{cal}=1\mid T_i,G_i), \quad
Z_i\notin\operatorname{inputs}(C_i).
\end{equation}
Here $y^{cal}$ contains training ground-truth positives and high-margin counterfactual
negatives, not direct human validity. We denote family-conditioned and pooled
implementations by \pgeomfam{} and \pgeom{}. Predicate-aligned transformations
are $T_i\times G_i$ interactions rather than raw geometry; neither $Z_i$ nor
source identity enters the calibrator. Final scores use $S_i=F(Z_i,C_i)$, with
product and rank-average as soft instantiations rather than proofs or a
universal formula.

Let $L_K(c)$ be the top-$K$ measured-family rows for context $c$ and $Y_c$ the
exact-label ground-truth set. We report
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{R@K}=\frac{\sum_c |L_K(c)\cap Y_c|}{\sum_c |Y_c|}.
\end{equation}
Family mapping determines only the geometry-checkable scope; it never relaxes
predicate-label matching. Let $D_K$ contain the selected rows whose verifier
status is satisfied, uncertain, or violated. The primary diagnostic is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{V@K}_{all}=\frac{1}{|D_K|}
\sum_{(c,i)\in D_K}\mathbf{1}[v_i=\mathrm{violated}].
\end{equation}
Because uncertain rows lower this ratio without being declared satisfied, we
also report decidable-only violation, uncertainty rate, and a pessimistic bound
that counts every uncertain row as violated. Rows without a supported verifier
status affect status coverage but no violation denominator. Recall, violation,
uncertainty, and coverage are interpreted jointly.
