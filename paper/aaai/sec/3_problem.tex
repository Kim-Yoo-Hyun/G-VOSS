\section{Problem Formulation}

Let a relation source produce a set of candidate relation rows for a 3D scene.
Each row is written as
\begin{equation}
r_i = (\mathrm{scan}_i,\mathrm{subgraph}_i,s_i,o_i,p_i,a_i,\mathrm{score}^{sem}_i),
\end{equation}
where $s_i$ and $o_i$ are subject and object instance identifiers, $p_i$ is the
predicate label, $a_i$ is the mapped predicate family, and
$\mathrm{score}^{sem}_i$ is the source semantic confidence. The tuple
$(\mathrm{scan}_i,\mathrm{subgraph}_i,s_i,o_i)$ is the identity key used to
preserve the object pair across prediction, geometry, and ground-truth joins.

For each prediction row, we join object-pair geometry evidence
\begin{equation}
g_i = G(\mathrm{scan}_i,\mathrm{subgraph}_i,s_i,o_i).
\end{equation}
The evidence can include OBB features, point/local contact evidence, distance,
vertical ordering, support/contact subtype information, and missing-evidence
reason codes. \method{} evaluates only geometry-checkable relation families:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{A}=\{&\texttt{support\_contact},\texttt{proximity},\\
              &\texttt{relative\_vertical}\}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Rows outside these families are preserved in exports but excluded from the measured
violation claim.

The geometry verifier assigns a row-level status
$v_i \in \{\mathrm{satisfied},\mathrm{uncertain},\mathrm{violated}\}$, and the
calibrator estimates
\begin{equation}
\pgeom_i = P(v_i \neq \mathrm{violated} \mid g_i,a_i).
\end{equation}
The score is a calibrated reliability signal, not a proof of correctness. For
this reason, the paper reports probabilistic, rule-verified, and
family-specific operating points separately.

The main recall metric is exact predicate-label \rAt{K} over the fixed in-scope
ground-truth denominator. Family grouping defines the geometry-checkable
reliability scope and violation analysis; it does not relax predicate-label
matching. \vAt{K} is the fraction of top-$K$ in-scope predicted rows whose
joined geometry is judged violated. Top-$K$ is formed per evaluated
subgraph/source ranking after the measured-family filter; satisfied, uncertain, and
violated rows form the violation denominator, while unsupported or missing-join
rows are excluded from that denominator and reported through coverage caveats.
Only rows with verification status \texttt{violated} count as violations. We
report recall retention and violation reduction together so that violation
reduction cannot be interpreted without its recall tradeoff.
