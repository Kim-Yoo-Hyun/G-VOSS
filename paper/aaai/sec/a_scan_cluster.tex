\section{Scan-Cluster Confidence Intervals}

The analysis resamples the 157 scan identifiers 1,000 times and carries
every context of a sampled scan together. The ranking, model, and point
estimates remain unchanged; shared bootstrap indices are used across
conditions. A paired 548-context bootstrap gives the same directions and is
retained as a finer-grained sensitivity.

At $K=50$, every Verifier-V interval remains below zero. Recall improves on
Open3DSG and SGFN, while the near-saturated VL-SAT interval is centered near
zero. At $K=100$, the changes have the same direction and strictly positive
Recall intervals for all three predictors; its point estimates remain in the
main five-budget table.
