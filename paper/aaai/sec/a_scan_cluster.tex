\section{Scan-Cluster Bootstrap Sensitivity}

The main analysis resamples 548 relation contexts. To test dependence among
contexts from the same scene, we additionally resample the 157 scan identifiers
1,000 times and carry every context of a sampled scan together. The ranking,
model, and point estimates remain unchanged; shared bootstrap indices are used
across conditions.
