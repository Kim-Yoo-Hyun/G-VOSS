\section{Strong-Comparator and Open3DSG Sensitivities}

\paragraph{Supervision-matched nonlinearity.}
To separate architecture capacity from supervision, we fit a 69-parameter
two-hidden-unit MLP using the same 60,208 training rows, constructed
compatibility target, feature contract, source-score exclusion, and
1,061/117/157 split as the linear model. It is fitted once and applied
unchanged to all three predictors. Table~\ref{tab:nonlinear-comparison} also
shows a separate nonlinear rescorer trained on stronger SGFN-specific
exact-label correctness. Open3DSG uses the complete-coverage sensitivity here
so every comparator sees the same candidate set.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\scriptsize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
\caption{\textbf{Nonlinear comparison at $K=100$.} Cells are Recall / verifier
V. The matched MLP does not dominate the linear structured product; the last
column uses stronger SGFN-specific supervision.}
\label{tab:nonlinear-comparison}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Source & Linear product & Matched MLP & Exact-label MLP \\
\midrule
VL-SAT & .9688/.0325 & .9688/.0317 & .9625/.0311 \\
Open3DSG & .6055/.0339 & .6415/.0434 & .6166/.0334 \\
SGFN & .9418/.0372 & .9421/.0363 & .9466/.0279 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Against the linear product, the matched MLP's paired Open3DSG changes are
$+.0360$ Recall [.0252,.0475] and $+.0096$ V [.0079,.0113]. On VL-SAT and
SGFN, Recall differences are within $\pm.0003$ and V differences are at most
.0010 in magnitude for this structured MLP. The stronger exact-label model is
best on its SGFN source but is worse than routed \method{} on both VL-SAT
metrics and exhibits a Recall--V trade-off on Open3DSG. This supports the
factorized compatibility mechanism, not universal formula superiority.

\paragraph{Open3DSG public-route sensitivity.}
The public preprocessing drops a context when fewer than four annotated
objects retain view metadata. This accounts for all 15 missing contexts.
Table~\ref{tab:open3dsg-routes} distinguishes the faithful eligible-context
evaluation, the conservative full-target main evaluation, and a documented
full-coverage recovery.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\scriptsize
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{3pt}
\caption{\textbf{Open3DSG route sensitivity at $K=100$.} Cells are Recall /
verifier V. The main route uses the full 3,972-relation denominator and no
predictions for the 15 public-pipeline drops.}
\label{tab:open3dsg-routes}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Route & Contexts / GT & Source & Routed \method{} \\
\midrule
Public eligible & 533 / 3,899 & .5206/.1242 & .5799/.0324 \\

\end{table}

The conclusion is stable to all three denominator and coverage choices. The
main paper uses the conservative full-target public route; recovered results
are sensitivity evidence rather than the headline benchmark.
