\section{Limitations}

The claim covers contact/support, proximity, and vertical ordering, where
reconstructed geometry supports falsifiable checks. Functional and pragmatic
predicates remain outside scope. Front/behind is frame-ambiguous, and a
left/right variant fails its train/dev strict gate; these are reported as
negative scope evidence. Exact-label GT-object evaluation also makes Open3DSG
relation-reliability evidence rather than full open-vocabulary generation.

Open3DSG uses a selected non-averaged checkpoint and documented 548/548 recovery
branch, not an official leaderboard reproduction. The 533/548 route and
historical 377/388--388/388 comparison are sensitivity evidence; broad SOTA and
arbitrary-source generality remain blocked.

V is verifier-derived; two blinded Codex passes share a model family and lack
human alignment, so they are proxy diagnostics. Fusion is source-dependent:
\texttt{support\_contact} V regresses, while on untouched 11-scene
ReplicaSSG/FROSS, product is unchanged at $K=100$ and rank-average fails the
recall guardrail (supplement). Thus we claim no formula, family, or dataset-wide
generality.

SGFN preserves two pre-inference errata for split identity and the
\texttt{full\_l160} URL. Eleven self-\texttt{supported by} GT rows lack model
edges; no predictions are synthesized, and all remain in the 3,972-row recall
denominator. The scores remain constructed-target reliability, not human
validity; factor controls do not expand SGFN or support a generic interaction
claim. Future work requires human alignment, broader relations, and embodied
tests.
