\section{Limitations}

GeoCalib assumes a geometry-identifiable predicate and known object instances.
We test support/contact, proximity, and vertical order; functional predicates
remain out of scope, front/behind is frame-dependent, and left/right fails the
defined controls. The task is relation reliability, not complete open-vocabulary
graph generation.

Violation@$K$ is verifier-derived, and its geometric primitives overlap with
the compatibility target. Counterfactual controls test the learned factor but
do not replace an independent physical-validity reference. Moreover,
support/contact regresses, and the initial ReplicaSSG/FROSS transfer exposes
source-score scale, ranking-displacement, geometry-representation, and ontology
shift. ReplicaSSG is used as a development diagnostic rather than an unbiased
external evaluation: bounded fusion preserves recall in an all-scene fit but
fails the leave-one-scene-out recall guardrail. The evidence therefore establishes source-level
generalization on one 3DSSG target, not uniform family or dataset-wide
generality.

A source-supervised nonlinear rescorer also outperforms the parameter-free
product when exact development labels are available. Our claim is thus
source-independent factor isolation, not formula optimality. Future work should
validate the metric independently, improve contact evidence, and test
downstream decision benefits.
