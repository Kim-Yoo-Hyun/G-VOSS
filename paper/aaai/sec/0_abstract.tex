\begin{abstract}
3D scene graph predictors can assign high scores to predicates that are inconsistent with the reconstructed geometry of the corresponding ordered subject--object pair. We formulate predicate--geometry compatibility for fixed relation predictions and introduce RelCompat3D, a re-ranking framework. RelCompat3D preserves the ordered-pair identity of each candidate and estimates compatibility from predicate semantics and predicate-independent pair measurements without using the source relation score. Family-specific linear heads and a shared nonlinear estimator are trained on linked ground-truth positives and constructed counterfactuals. Transformation averaging assigns the same compatibility to equivalent endpoint and predicate representations. RelCompat3D combines compatibility with the source relation score to re-rank proximity and vertical-order candidates while retaining support/contact candidates in source order. We evaluate exact-match Recall@$K$ and verifier-derived Violation@$K$ for VL-SAT, Open3DSG, and SceneGraphFusion (SGFN) on a shared 3D Semantic Scene Graph (3DSSG) validation set. Across all reported predictor--$K$ settings, both variants yield Recall point estimates no lower and Violation point estimates no higher than the corresponding source rankings. The largest changes occur on Open3DSG. Matched fusion baselines and controls show predictor- and family-dependent trade-offs. A point- and mesh-based audit supports the direction of the changes using alternative geometric measurements.
\end{abstract}
