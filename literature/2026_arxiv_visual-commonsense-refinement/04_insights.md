# Insights

## Facts

- The method is post-hoc and model-agnostic with respect to the neural SGG
  source.
- It mines spatial, functional, symmetry, inverse, and composition constraints.
- It introduces/reports Constraint Violation Rate alongside task metrics.

## Paper Claims

The authors claim practical transfer across image-SGG datasets and
architectures without retraining the source model.

## Inferences

- Relation algebra cannot be H001's novelty by itself.
- H001's strongest remaining method distinction is the combined contract:
  source-excluded continuous 3D compatibility, ordered-pair identity,
  counterfactual margin training, exact algebra projection, and uncertainty-
  aware recall/violation evaluation.
- The shared circularity risk across both papers makes independent physical
  validity, not a new verifier rule, the strongest future validation.

## Connection to Field Trends

Scene-graph reliability is moving from implicit learned scores toward explicit
constraint/refinement layers and separate consistency metrics. This raises the
novelty bar for H001 but also validates the importance of the failure class.

## Possible Contribution Angles

- Soft 3D compatibility rather than global hard rule satisfaction.
- Observability-aware uncertainty for incomplete reconstructed geometry.
- Source-factor isolation and cross-predictor transfer under fixed 3D geometry.

## What Would Change This Assessment

A later version demonstrating continuous source-excluded 3D compatibility,
same-pair identity controls, or independent physical-validity evaluation would
substantially narrow H001's remaining distinction.
